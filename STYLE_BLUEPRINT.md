# WAVE PAY & STRATEGIES — PROJECT STYLE BLUEPRINT

## Overall Feeling

A luxury, underwater-inspired experience — calm, fluid, and premium. The mood is "deep ocean at night": dark, confident, and luminous, with light filtering through water. Everything should feel like it's gently in motion (waves, bubbles, shimmer) but never chaotic. The impression is trust, intelligence, and effortless flow — a high-end business partner, not a generic tech vendor.

## Design Personality

- Aquatic metaphor throughout: words like "flow," "currents," "seamless," "deep," "tide"
- Confident and minimal, but rich in atmosphere (depth, glow, subtle texture)
- Editorial luxury meets modern fintech — serif elegance paired with crisp sans precision

## Color Direction

- Backgrounds: very deep navy-black (`#030810`, `#060c18`, `#0c1828`)
- Primary accent: bright teal/turquoise (`#2ab8d4`) with a glow variant (`#4dd9ef`)
- Secondary warmth: muted gold (`#c9a96e`) used sparingly
- Text: soft white (`rgba(255,255,255,0.86)`), dim labels (`rgba(255,255,255,0.62)`)
- Borders: faint teal (`rgba(42,184,212,0.14)`), brighter on hover

## Typography

- Headings: Cormorant Garamond serif, light weight (300), italic for emphasis — thin and elegant
- Body & labels: Montserrat sans, uppercase labels with wide letter-spacing (0.2–0.32em)
- Strong size contrast: large delicate serif titles vs. small precise uppercase labels

## Motion & Effects

- Scroll-reveal fade-ups (slow, 0.9s ease)
- Subtle ambient animation: wave canvas, rising bubbles, caustic light shimmer
- Hover: gentle lift + teal glow shadow, never aggressive
- Floating glassmorphic stat cards with backdrop blur

## Form / Intake Sheet Treatment

- Card on a translucent dark surface (`rgba(8,18,32,0.65)`), 1px teal border, 14px radius
- Inputs: near-transparent dark fill, teal focus ring + glow
- Labels: tiny uppercase, wide-spaced, dim
- Primary button: solid turquoise with dark text, lifts and glows on hover
- Success state: teal-tinted confirmation panel
- Mobile: solid opaque dropdown panels so text stays readable over dark backgrounds

## Brand Rules

- Always "Wave Pay & Strategies" — never use a dash separator
- No "financing" offerings
- Founder: Noah Nodal · "Smart Solutions. Stronger Businesses."
